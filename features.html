  • Multi-zone layouts for simultaneous content areas

Content is pre-downloaded and cached on the local device. When the internet goes down, your screens keep playing — ensuring uninterrupted display in locations with unstable connectivity.

In critical situations, instantly override all running content on one or all screens with an emergency message. No delays, no manual intervention at each device.
