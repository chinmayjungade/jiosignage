Questions, answered

What hardware do I need to get started?
Any Android, Windows, web-capable or SoC smart display works. You can use screens and media players you already own — just install the JioSignage player or open the web player, pair with a code, and you're live.
Can screens keep playing if the internet goes down?
Yes. Content is cached locally on each device, so scheduled playback continues uninterrupted during network outages and syncs automatically once connectivity returns.
How does JioSignage scale across many locations?
The platform is multi-tenant and built for thousands of screens. Group displays by region, brand or franchisee, delegate access with roles, and publish to hundreds of locations in a single action.
Is my data and network secure?
Content is encrypted end-to-end, sign-in supports SSO via SAML/OAuth, and every action is captured in audit logs. Role-based governance ensures teams only access what they should.
Can it integrate with our existing systems?
Open REST APIs let you automate publishing and connect JioSignage to your POS, DAM, data feeds and internal tools. Live web, RSS and data-driven content are supported out of the box.
What kind of support is included?
Enterprise plans include 24/7 support, guided onboarding, an uptime SLA and a dedicated customer success manager to help you roll out and scale.
